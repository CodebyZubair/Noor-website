About Noor

Noor is a website for the day's prayers: Fajr, sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha, plus a qibla compass, a Quran reader, and reminders. It opens in English, German, Urdu, Arabic, and Turkish. The default city is Lahore. Germany, Austria, and Switzerland use Diyanet. There is no account.

Open today's prayer times

Not those other products

Noor is independent of diegebetszeiten.de and of Islam360. diegebetszeiten.de often shows an earlier Shafi-style Asr. Noor's Pakistan default is the Karachi method with Hanafi Asr, which is intentionally later. For Germany, Austria, and Switzerland the default is Diyanet, method 13, and standard Asr, school 0.

Islam360 is a store app. It can keep offline packs and it uses an account. Noor does not. Reminders here run only while the page is open. If you need a sound on a locked phone, use the phone's own alarm.

What you can do here

Change city, use the location button, read a month, mark the five prayers, count tasbeeh, open the 99 names, keep a short dua list, and read hadith chapters as they stream. The Quran and the qibla each have their own page. Questions are on the FAQ. Storage is described on privacy. The German landing page is Gebetszeiten.
